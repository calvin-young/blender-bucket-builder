# Challenge buckets: what to send with each

Hand-packed builds that were hard to make are the best test cases the packer
and Jiggle have. Each one is kept locally (never in this repo); this page is
the questionnaire that goes with it. Answer what you can; "don't know" is fine.

1. **Files:** the clashed version (sent first, for a blind test) and, after the
   report, your solved version.
2. **Parts:** how many of each, and any ratio rule (for example "every part
   7 times, 1:1", or kits).
3. **Clearances:** the minimum you accept, what is acceptable, what you prefer,
   and whether more is better.
4. **Compromises:** anything you accepted below the minimum (how close, over
   how large an area) and whether it printed fine.
5. **May parts turn?** Which parts must keep their orientation (surface finish,
   supports, a face that must be up)?
6. **Part quality versus count:** would you rather drop a copy, or keep every
   copy with compromises?
7. **What was hard:** the spot or the shapes that made it difficult.
8. **How you solved it:** which tool, roughly how long, what moves (nudges,
   turns, re-seating parts elsewhere).
9. **Printer and build:** printer model and anything unusual about the job.
